Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Nilo: your data stays on your computer

Projects, tasks, notes and budget are saved in a SQLite database (nilo.db) in the folder you choose. Backups, manual or automatic, are written locally in that same folder. Content created in Nilo is never sent to the publisher.

2. What Nilo sends online

  • Business licence: when activating and verifying, the licence key and IP address are sent to Polar.
  • Updates: at startup, Nilo reads a public latest.json file to see whether a new version exists. No personal data is sent.
  • Feedback: only if you click "Report" or "Suggest", which opens a pre-filled form or message; you stay in control of what you send.

3. Buying the Business licence

Order data (name, e-mail, country, payment details) is collected and processed by Polar, acting as Merchant of Record. The publisher has access to it to manage licences, support and refunds, and uses it for no other purpose, in particular not for marketing. Polar processes this data under its own privacy policy.

4. This website

The publisher places no cookies or trackers on this site, which loads no third-party resources (fonts are hosted on the site). Only your choice of light or dark theme is remembered in your browser's local storage; it is never transmitted. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (see the legal notice). Download links lead to GitHub, which applies its own privacy policy.

5. Legal bases and retention

  • Business licence: performance of the contract (licence activation and verification, support).
  • Updates: the publisher's legitimate interest in distributing up-to-date versions.
  • Retention: for as long as needed to manage the licence, then as required by Polar's legal obligations.

6. Transfers outside the European Union

Polar and GitHub are providers located outside the European Union: the data entrusted to them (order and licence data for Polar, hosting of the site and downloads for GitHub) may therefore be processed outside the European Union, under their own policies.

7. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification and erasure, and the other rights provided by the GDPR, by writing to contact@vinxlabs.fr. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority.

8. Controller
