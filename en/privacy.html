Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers the vinxlabs.fr website and the Nilo app (“Nilo Project” on the Microsoft Store). Data controller: Vincent ROMEO, VinX Labs, 2 boulevard du 14 Juillet, 13500 Martigues, France — contact@vinxlabs.fr.

1. In short

Your projects, tasks and files stay on your computer, in the folder you choose (your “vault”). VinX Labs never has access to them: Nilo has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics.

2. What Nilo sends over the Internet

Nilo only connects to the Internet in these cases:

  • Business licence (if you buy one): on activation and during periodic checks, Nilo sends Lemon Squeezy the licence key, a device name (“Nilo — Windows”) and, like any connection, your IP address. The purchase itself (name, e-mail, country, payment) is handled by Lemon Squeezy, the official reseller, under its privacy policy.
  • Updates: the Microsoft Store version is updated by the Microsoft Store; the version downloaded from this site checks GitHub (IP address, installed version).
  • Feedback and error reports, only when you choose to: an e-mail to contact@vinxlabs.fr (prepared by Nilo, which you review before sending; it contains the Nilo and Windows versions and the technical error details) or the Google Forms form.

3. The vinxlabs.fr website

The site sets no cookies and uses no trackers. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address to serve the pages. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the Nilo page queries GitHub to show the latest version number: these services also receive your IP address.

4. Data VinX Labs keeps

For Business licence buyers: the order details shown in Lemon Squeezy (name, e-mail, country, key), to manage licences, support and refunds, for the legal retention period of billing records (10 years). For people who e-mail support: their e-mails, for as long as needed to handle the request. This data is never sold or used for marketing.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (licence, support) and legal obligations (invoicing).

5. Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by e-mailing contact@vinxlabs.fr. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

6. Changes

This page may change as Nilo evolves; the date at the top shows the last update.
