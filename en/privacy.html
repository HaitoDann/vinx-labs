Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy covers the vinxlabs.fr website and the Nilo app (“Nilo Project” on the Microsoft Store). Data controller: Vincent ROMEO, VinX Labs, 2 boulevard du 14 Juillet, 13500 Martigues, France — contact@vinxlabs.fr.

1. In short

Your projects, tasks and files stay on your computer, in the folder you choose (your “vault”). VinX Labs never has access to them: Nilo has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics.

2. What Nilo sends over the Internet

Nilo only connects to the Internet in these cases:

  • Business license (if you buy one): on activation and during periodic checks, Nilo sends Polar the license key, a device name (“Nilo — Windows”) and, like any connection, your IP address. The purchase itself (name, e-mail, country, payment) is handled by Polar, the official reseller, under its privacy policy.
  • Updates: the Microsoft Store version is updated by the Microsoft Store; the version downloaded from this site checks GitHub (IP address, installed version).
  • Feedback and error reports, only when you choose to: an e-mail to contact@vinxlabs.fr (prepared by Nilo, which you review before sending; it contains the Nilo and Windows versions and the technical error details) or the Google Forms form.

3. The vinxlabs.fr website

The publisher places no cookies or trackers on this site, which loads no third-party resources (fonts are hosted on the site). It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address to serve the pages. Only your choice of light or dark theme is remembered in your browser's local storage; it is never transmitted. Download links lead to GitHub, which applies its own privacy policy.

4. Data VinX Labs keeps

For Business license buyers: the order details shown in Polar (name, e-mail, country, key), to manage licenses, support and refunds, for the legal retention period of billing records (10 years). For people who e-mail support: their e-mails, for as long as needed to handle the request. This data is never sold or used for marketing.

Legal bases: performance of the contract (license, support), legal obligations (invoicing) and, for update checks, the publisher's legitimate interest in distributing up-to-date versions.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Polar and GitHub are providers located outside the European Union: the data entrusted to them (order and license data for Polar; hosting of the site, downloads and updates for GitHub) may therefore be processed outside the European Union, under their own policies.

6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by e-mailing contact@vinxlabs.fr. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

7. Changes

This page may change as Nilo evolves; the date at the top shows the last update.
