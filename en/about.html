About VinX Labs

VinX Labs is a French software studio founded by Vincent ROMEO.

Why VinX Labs

I created VinX Labs to offer simple, well-designed software, and to push back against outrageous prices and subscriptions for everything.

In a few years, I would like VinX Labs to become a small start-up: around ten carefully made programs, and a small community that likes the studio’s philosophy.

The founder

Vincent ROMEO, founder of VinX Labs. IT engineer trained at CESI (GMSI, then ASR, then MICSI), I have been developing software for three years.
